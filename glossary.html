Advanced Stats & Glossary

Welcome to the Spartan Advanced Stats Hub guide! Here is a breakdown of what our metrics and dashboard sections mean.

EPA (Expected Points Added)

What it is: Measures the value of a play based on down, distance, and field position relative to scoring expectations.

Significance: Positive EPA means a play successfully moved MSU closer to scoring points.

Success Rate

What it is: A binary metric checking whether a play gained enough yardage to stay on schedule (50% on 1st down, 70% on 2nd down, 100% on 3rd/4th down).

Significance: High success rate proves consistent, reliable offense.

Explosiveness

What it is: Measures how productive a team's successful chunk plays are.

Significance: Highlights big-play capability downfield or on long runs.

Win Probability (WP)

What it is: The real-time statistical chance of winning based on score, time remaining, and field position.

Significance: Tracks momentum swings and turning points across the game.

Key Player Impact & Usage

What it is: Tracks individual player stats (passing yards, rushing efficiency, target share) during games.

Significance: Identifies which playmakers are driving the team's offensive or defensive production.
